AnalytIQ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 20, 2026

AnalytIQ operates the AnalytIQ data analytics learning platform and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Service.

This policy is designed to comply with applicable privacy and education data protection laws, including FERPA, U.S. state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA, and applicable international data protection frameworks.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: We collect your name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form). We also store your role, such as student or instructor.
  • Profile Information: Optional profile details may include display name, biography, and profile image.
  • Class and Course Data: We store classes, enrollments, course materials, and related educational content uploaded to the Service.
  • Learning and Practice Data: We collect data related to your use of the platform, including progress, exercise results, scores, completion status, and time spent.
  • AI Feature Data: If you use AI features, we process prompts and responses temporarily to provide functionality. This data is not retained for training artificial intelligence models.
  • Support Communications: We collect information you provide when contacting support.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and Usage Data: We collect technical information such as IP address, device type, browser type, and usage patterns to operate and secure the Service.
  • Local Storage and Session Data: We use browser storage to maintain session state, preferences, and authentication tokens.

1.3 Information from Service Providers

We use service providers that process data on our behalf, including:

  • Supabase for hosting and database infrastructure
  • AWS cloud infrastructure via Supabase
  • Email providers for transactional communications
  • Google Gemini API for AI functionality
  • PostHog for product analytics and usage insights

These providers process data only as necessary to operate the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use data to:

  • Provide and operate the Service
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts
  • Track learning progress and display educational results
  • Provide AI assistance features
  • Send transactional communications such as password resets
  • Monitor system performance and security
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use personal information for advertising.

3. FERPA and Education Records

When the Service is used by educational institutions, AnalytIQ acts as a "school official" with a "legitimate educational interest" under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

Education records may include enrollment information, coursework, progress data, and instructor feedback.

AnalytIQ processes such records solely on behalf of institutions and under their direction.

AnalytIQ does not own student education records, and all rights remain with the educational institution and student.

Student data is never sold or used for marketing.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We share data only in limited circumstances:

  • With service providers that operate the platform
  • With instructors regarding student learning progress
  • When required by law
  • In connection with business transfers

All service providers are contractually required to protect data.

5. Analytics and Tracking

We use platform analytics tools to understand system usage and improve functionality.

These tools collect non-advertising usage data such as feature interactions, session events, and performance metrics.

We do not use tracking for advertising or cross-site behavioral profiling.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the Service.

If you request account deletion, your personal data is permanently deleted from active systems.

Educational institutions may retain records separately within their own systems.

System logs are retained according to default Supabase retention periods.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission using HTTPS
  • Encryption at rest
  • Role-based access controls
  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Authentication protections

No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. AI Data Handling

AI features process user prompts only to generate real-time responses.

AnalytIQ does not store AI prompts for training purposes and does not use student data to train artificial intelligence models.

9. International Data Transfers

AnalytIQ operates from the United States and hosts data on infrastructure located in the United States.

By using the Service, international users consent to the transfer and processing of their data in the United States.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal data

Students using the Service through an educational institution should direct requests related to education records to their institution.

11. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

12. California Privacy Rights

California residents may request information regarding personal data collected, request deletion, and exercise rights under applicable privacy laws.

We do not sell or share personal information for advertising purposes.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact us through the information provided on our website.
