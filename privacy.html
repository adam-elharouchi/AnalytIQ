Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 17, 2025

AnalytIQ (“we,” “our,” or “us”) operates the AnalytIQ data analytics learning platform and related services (the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data. It is designed to meet common U.S. privacy expectations and to align with applicable laws, including where relevant the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the CPRA, and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your full name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form). We may also store a role (e.g., student or professor) associated with your account.
  • Profile information: If you use profile features, we may store your display name, email, a short bio, and a profile picture (e.g., uploaded image stored as data we maintain).
  • Preferences and settings: Such as email notification preferences and dark mode or other display settings you choose.
  • Class and course data: If you are a professor, we store classes you create (e.g., name, description, class code). If you are a student, we store your enrollments in classes. We may also store course materials (e.g., PDFs) you upload or link to, which can be stored on our systems or with our service providers.
  • Learning and practice data: We store your practice and exercise progress, including scores, accuracy, time spent, completion status, and attempt history, so we can provide the Service and show progress to you and, where applicable, to instructors.
  • Code assistant and AI features: If you use our code assistant or other AI features, we may store conversation history (e.g., messages and titles), AI-related preferences (e.g., language, model choice), and related context (e.g., goals, constraints, or memory items) to provide and improve those features.
  • Contact and support: If you contact us (e.g., through our contact form), we collect the information you submit, such as name, email, subject, and message content.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and usage data: When you use the Service, we may collect information such as IP address, browser type, device type, and general usage information (e.g., pages visited, features used) to operate the Service, prevent abuse, and improve our platform.
  • Local storage and session data: We use browser local storage and session storage to keep you signed in (e.g., authentication token, user identifier, profile snapshot), store preferences (e.g., language, AI model), and maintain session state (e.g., study progress, conversation history cache). This data stays in your browser unless you clear it or we describe otherwise.

1.3 Information From Third-Party Services

We use a limited set of service providers who may process data on our behalf:

  • Hosting and database: Account, profile, class, progress, and similar data are stored on our (or our providers’) servers.
  • File storage: Uploaded files (e.g., PDFs for courses) may be stored with our storage provider (e.g., Supabase) to deliver the Service.
  • Email: We use an email service to send you transactional messages (e.g., password reset links). Those providers process your email address and message content as necessary to deliver the emails.
  • Error and performance monitoring: We may use a service such as Sentry for error and performance monitoring. We configure it to avoid sending personally identifiable information (e.g., we filter sensitive headers and do not send PII by default).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account, authenticate you, and provide the Service.
  • Store and display your profile, preferences, and settings.
  • Enroll you in classes, show course content, track and display your progress, and (where applicable) show progress to instructors.
  • Provide the code assistant and other AI features, including saving and retrieving conversations and preferences.
  • Send you transactional messages (e.g., password reset emails) and, if you have opted in, other notifications consistent with your choices.
  • Operate, secure, and improve the Service (e.g., debugging, analytics, preventing abuse).
  • Comply with law and protect our rights and the safety of users.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers: With vendors who help us run the Service (e.g., hosting, storage, email, error monitoring) under contracts that limit use to providing the Service and protecting your data.
  • Instructors: If you are a student in a class, we may share your progress and related learning data with the instructor(s) of that class so they can teach and evaluate.
  • Legal and safety: When required by law, or to protect our or others’ rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, sale of assets, or similar transaction, subject to the same privacy commitments.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage and session storage (and similar technologies) to keep you signed in, store preferences, and support features like study progress and conversation history. You can clear this data via your browser settings; doing so may log you out and reset some in-browser preferences. We do not use cookies or similar technologies for cross-site advertising or to sell your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this policy. We may retain some data longer where required by law (e.g., for legal or audit purposes) or for legitimate business needs (e.g., security, dispute resolution). Password reset tokens are short-lived and removed after use or expiry. You may request deletion of your account and associated data as described below.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including hashing passwords, using secure connections (e.g., HTTPS), and limiting access to personal information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct your information (e.g., via your profile or account settings, or by contacting us).
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated personal information, subject to legal or legitimate retention needs.
  • Opt-out of “sale” or “sharing”: We do not sell personal information. We do not “share” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If our practices change, we will update this policy and provide a way to opt out where required.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information: Where applicable (e.g., under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to limit our use of certain sensitive categories. We use sensitive information only as needed to provide the Service and as described here.

To exercise these rights, contact us via the Contact page or the email address provided there. We may need to verify your identity. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

8. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it in accordance with applicable law.

9. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the following applies in addition to the rest of this policy:

  • Categories of personal information we collect: Identifiers (e.g., name, email, IP address); account and profile data; commercial information (e.g., use of the Service); internet or device activity; inferences related to your use of the Service; and, if you provide it, sensitive personal information (e.g., account credentials).
  • Purposes: As described in Sections 2 and 3 above, including providing the Service, security, and legal compliance.
  • Disclosure: We may disclose these categories to service providers and, for students, to instructors, as described in Section 3. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Rights: You have the right to know, correct, and delete personal information, and to opt out of sale and limit use of sensitive personal information, as summarized in Section 7. To exercise these rights, contact us as described in Section 7. You may also designate an authorized agent to make requests on your behalf.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. If we make material changes, we may provide additional notice (e.g., on the Service or by email). Your continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy, except where otherwise required by law.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your privacy rights, please contact us via our Contact page or the contact information provided there. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.
